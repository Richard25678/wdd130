Wild River Rafting Co.

At Wild River Rafting Co., we bring people together to experience the thrill of the river while keeping safety and stewardship at the heart of every trip. Our mission is to create memorable, responsible outdoor adventures for families and thrill-seekers alike.

History

Wild River Rafting Co. began with a small group of local guides who wanted to share the beauty and excitement of the river with visitors from near and far. Our early trips were simple weekend outings, but the passion for the outdoors and the trust of our guests quickly grew.

Over the years, we have built a tradition of safety, teamwork, and respect for the waterways we love. From first-time paddlers to experienced adventurers, our guides help every guest enjoy a memorable and secure trip through the rapids.

Today, our company continues to serve the community with a focus on sustainable river practices, skilled instruction, and unforgettable family adventures.
